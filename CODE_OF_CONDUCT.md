# Community standards

We welcome people with different backgrounds, experience levels and perspectives.
Questions, small improvements and careful criticism are useful contributions.

- Discuss ideas and evidence with respect. Explain technical terms when helpful.
- Give actionable feedback about the work. Avoid personal attacks, harassment,
  discrimination, threats and unwanted contact.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish private communications or identifying information
  without permission, and never share another person's credentials or account data.
- Credit other people's work and respect their licenses.
- Do not use project spaces for spam, misleading performance claims or investment
  solicitation.

These standards apply to issues, pull requests, discussions and other spaces
maintained by this project. Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, lock
conversations or restrict participation, with a response proportionate to the
behavior. They should explain moderation decisions when doing so respects privacy.

To report a concern, use a maintainer's private contact listed on their GitHub
profile. If no private contact is available, ask for a private reporting channel
without posting sensitive details. Appeals should go to a maintainer who was not
involved in the original decision where possible.
